## The Talk

**Forget efficiency – Become more productive without the stress** (_Uwe Friedrichsen_)

Efficiency is the holy grail of modern corporate management – also in IT. 
Developers are expected to continuously become more efficient, to get done more in less time. 
Unfortunately, usually this is just a huge waste of time and talent, only leading to increased developer burnout rates.

In this session, we will examine the roots of the omnipresent efficiency obsession and why it does not make sense any more. 
We will discuss what we should do instead to become more productive. 
And we will add some recommendations how to counter the efficiency madness as a developer.

Let us become more productive without the stress!
