Forget efficiency – Become more productive without the stress

13.02.2025 · 18:30 codecentric Uwe Friedrichsen Eintritt frei

The Java User Group Berlin-Brandenburg and our partner codecentric AG invite you to the talk Forget efficiency – Become more productive without the stress by Uwe Friedrichsen on February the 13th.

The Talk

Forget efficiency – Become more productive without the stress (Uwe Friedrichsen)

Efficiency is the holy grail of modern corporate management – also in IT. Developers are expected to continuously become more efficient, to get done more in less time. Unfortunately, usually this is just a huge waste of time and talent, only leading to increased developer burnout rates.

In this session, we will examine the roots of the omnipresent efficiency obsession and why it does not make sense any more. We will discuss what we should do instead to become more productive. And we will add some recommendations how to counter the efficiency madness as a developer.

Let us become more productive without the stress!

The Speaker

UF
Uwe Friedrichsen is a longtime traveller in the world of IT. Dot Connector. Cartographer of uncharted territory. Keeper of timeless wisdom. Translator between floors. System design. Resilience. Sustainability. Dislikes long bios. Tries to make IT a (bit) better place.
